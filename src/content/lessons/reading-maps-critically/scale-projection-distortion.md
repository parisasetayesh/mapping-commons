> **Opening question:** What changes when the same phenomenon is viewed at a different scale or through a different projection?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Compare the same world data in two projections.
2. Identify preserved and distorted properties.
3. Separate viewing extent from data resolution and geographic aggregation.
4. Compare one dataset summarized by two boundary systems.
5. Choose a scale and projection based on purpose rather than familiarity.

## Critical pause

Which places, populations, or patterns appear important because of the chosen projection, extent, or aggregation?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners explain one projection distortion and one effect of changing geographic scale.
