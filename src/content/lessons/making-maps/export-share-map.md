> **Opening question:** Where will the map actually be viewed, and what must remain readable in that setting?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Define whether the output is for print, projection, report, slide, phone, or web.
2. Set dimensions, orientation, resolution, and file format.
3. Add title, legend, source, date, units, and necessary explanatory text.
4. Test the result at its actual viewing size and in grayscale or reduced color conditions.
5. Provide alt text or a structured text alternative.
6. Export and verify the final file outside the mapping software.

## Critical pause

Which audiences lose access when interaction, color, screen size, bandwidth, or specialized vocabulary is required?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners complete a display-specific review and revise at least one element after testing the exported file.
