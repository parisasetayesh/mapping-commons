> **Opening question:** Which visible differences belong to the data, and which are produced by your classification and design?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Confirm the selected field's definition, type, unit, and denominator.
2. Match single, categorized, or graduated symbols to the variable.
3. Compare classification methods and class counts.
4. Choose a color scheme and visual hierarchy suitable for the audience.
5. Test legend clarity, missing values, outlines, contrast, and color-independent cues.
6. Save the style and rationale.

## Critical pause

Does the design exaggerate difference, turn missing values into low values, or imply a ranking that the data does not support?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners compare two designs and justify the selected classification, color, and normalization.
