> **Opening question:** Which concepts remain the same across GIS platforms even when the buttons and panels change?

## Planned learning sequence

1. State the map purpose and audience.
2. Create a project and identify the catalog, map view, contents, and attribute table.
3. Add one documented layer and inspect geometry, attributes, extent, and coordinate system.
4. Apply basic symbology connected to the data's meaning.
5. Save the project and record source and limitation notes.

## Critical pause

How do licensing, account access, institutional infrastructure, and sharing permissions affect who can reproduce or maintain the project?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners explain the difference among the ArcGIS project, map, layer, and underlying dataset.
