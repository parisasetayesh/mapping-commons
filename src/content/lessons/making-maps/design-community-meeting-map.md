> **Opening question:** Can every participant understand and respond to the map without controlling the dashboard interface?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Identify the meeting purpose, participants, languages, room, devices, time, and facilitation roles.
2. Select only the layers and indicators needed for the discussion.
3. Prepare projected, printed, mobile, and text-based versions as appropriate.
4. Translate definitions and uncertainty into plain language.
5. Plan questions, annotation methods, and ways to record participant knowledge.
6. Explain what will happen to the information after the meeting.

## Critical pause

Does the map structure the meeting around institutional data, or does the meeting allow participants to redefine the question and evidence?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners test the package from the back of the room, on a phone, and without interaction, then revise it.
