> **Opening question:** Is this boundary part of the phenomenon, or simply the container used to report it?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Compare census, postal, municipal, service, watershed, floodplain, and community-defined boundaries.
2. Identify the institutional purpose of each geography.
3. Examine how the same data appears when aggregated differently.
4. Check boundary dates and changes over time.
5. Choose a geography based on question, process, audience, privacy, and available evidence.

## Critical pause

Which communities, relationships, or environmental processes are divided, merged, or made invisible by the chosen boundary?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners justify one boundary choice and name one important mismatch it creates.
