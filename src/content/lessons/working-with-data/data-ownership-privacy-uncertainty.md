> **Opening question:** Just because data can be mapped publicly, should it be—and who gets to decide?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Identify the data owner, steward, license, access conditions, and correction process.
2. Determine whether locations identify or expose people, households, facilities, or community resources.
3. Review aggregation, masking, access restriction, and omission options.
4. Identify sampling, measurement, model, reporting, and missing-data uncertainty.
5. Draft public language that communicates limits without making the map unusable.

## Critical pause

Who bears the risk of disclosure, misclassification, or uncertainty, and who receives the benefit of publication?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners produce a responsible-use note and identify one decision that requires consultation beyond the mapping team.
