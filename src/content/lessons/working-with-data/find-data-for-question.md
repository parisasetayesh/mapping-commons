> **Opening question:** Which organization would create this information as part of its ordinary work—and what would it call the dataset?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Extract search terms for phenomenon, population, geography, and time.
2. Identify agencies, research institutions, libraries, community organizations, and public portals likely to hold the data.
3. Search for datasets and documentation rather than only maps or PDFs.
4. Compare source authority, coverage, format, update schedule, and license.
5. Create a shortlist before downloading.

## Critical pause

Which forms of knowledge are absent because no institution collects them, or because collection would be extractive, unsafe, or inappropriate?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners compare three candidate sources and explain which one deserves closer metadata review.
