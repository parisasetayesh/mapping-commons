> **Opening question:** Does the map show where there is more of the phenomenon—or simply where there are more people, households, roads, or acres?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Map or inspect the raw count.
2. Identify possible populations or exposure measures for a denominator.
3. Calculate a percentage, rate, or density with matching units and periods.
4. Compare the visible patterns.
5. Decide whether the audience needs count, normalized measure, or both.

## Critical pause

Who appears more or less burdened under each denominator, and what responsibility disappears if only relative rates or only totals are shown?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners state the numerator, denominator, population, period, unit, and interpretive tradeoff.
