> **Opening question:** Which population, period, estimate, and boundary does this Census variable actually describe?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Distinguish decennial enumeration from ACS estimates.
2. Identify one-year versus five-year periods and intended uses.
3. Locate the universe, estimate, margin of error, and variable definition.
4. Select a compatible geographic level and boundary vintage.
5. Preserve and use the GEOID as a text identifier.

## Critical pause

How do survey uncertainty, confidentiality, category definitions, and geographic aggregation affect claims about communities?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners document one selected variable, its universe, period, geography, GEOID, and uncertainty measure.
