> **Opening question:** Whose work and authority are hidden behind the name printed on a map?

## Planned learning sequence

1. Identify the visible author or sponsoring organization.
2. Trace data collectors, funders, software providers, analysts, designers, and represented communities.
3. Distinguish stated purpose from likely institutional uses.
4. Examine ownership, licensing, maintenance, and correction processes.
5. Build a small actor-and-decision diagram for the selected map.

## Critical pause

Which contributors receive credit, which remain invisible, and who is able to contest an error or category?

## Planned checkpoint

Learners name at least four actors behind the map and connect each to one decision, resource, or form of authority.
